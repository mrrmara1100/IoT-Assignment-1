PUT YOUR POP-UP GIFS AND PHOTOS HERE
====================================

Accepted files: .gif (animated), .png, .jpg, .jpeg, .webp, .bmp
If a folder has several files, one is picked at random each time.
If a folder is empty, a text card is shown instead.

expressions/   smile, surprise, wink, kiss, angry, sad
head/          look_left, look_right, look_up, look_down, tilt_left, tilt_right
gestures/      thumbs_up, thumbs_down, victory, fist, open_palm, pointing_up, i_love_you
postures/      any clear photo of a person in a posture (no sub-folders)

POSTURES
- Each photo in postures/ is both the pose to copy and the picture that pops up.
- The whole body (or at least head, shoulders and arms) must be visible in the photo.
- Photos where no body is found are skipped (a message is printed in the terminal).
- Press C in the app (with posture on) to save the camera image as a new posture photo.
- Measurements are stored in cache/pose_refs.json; delete it to re-measure all photos.

Only use images you are allowed to use. Self-collected images are fine for personal
or school projects; check the rights before publishing the project.
